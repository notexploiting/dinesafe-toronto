    About this dashboard

    Data

    Inspection records come from the City of Toronto's DineSafe open dataset (about the last three years, refreshed daily). Older records (2001 to 2023) come from the same dataset's historical archive and are shown for establishments that can be linked to it. The pipeline merges establishments that the City's late-2025 system migration listed twice, and removes the duplicated inspections.

    Pass, Conditional Pass and Closed are Toronto Public Health's official results. After a Conditional Pass or Closed result, the City re-inspects, usually within a few days. Establishments that were not carried into the City's new system in November 2025 are hidden by default because they have most likely closed.

    The archive rarely records Conditional Pass or Closed results, so older statuses mostly read "Pass". Infractions and their severities in the archive are reliable.

    Predictions

    For each establishment, a logistic regression model estimates the chance that its next routine inspection finds (a) any infraction, (b) a significant or crucial infraction and (c) a result that is not a Pass. It uses only the establishment's past inspection record: how often and how recently it had infractions, what kinds, how often it is inspected, the City's risk tier where known, its category and whether it is a chain. On the most recent 12 months of inspections, which the model did not see in training, its ROC AUC was around 0.7. That means it ranks establishments much better than chance, but it is far from certain about any single one.

    Risk bands compare the chance of a significant or crucial infraction with the city-wide rate: Low < 0.75×, Average 0.75 to 1.25×, Elevated 1.25 to 2×, High ≥ 2×.

    These are statistical estimates, not official ratings. A high score does not mean that an establishment is unsafe today. For the official result, check the latest inspection status.

    Categories

    The current feed no longer says what kind of business an establishment is. The category comes from the historical archive where possible. Otherwise it is predicted from the establishment's name and marked "inferred".
