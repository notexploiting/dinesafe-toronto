# DineSafe risk model card

_Generated by `python -m dinesafe build`; numbers refer to the data snapshot `dinesafe_20261113_125541.csv` (inspections 2023-11-10 to 2026-11-12)._

## What it predicts

For each establishment, the probability that its **next routine inspection** will have:

* **any infraction** (`p_any`)
* **a significant or crucial infraction** (`p_serious`), which also drives the risk band
* **a Conditional Pass or Closed result** (`p_not_pass`)

Follow-up re-inspections (within 30 days after a Conditional Pass or Closed) are not prediction targets. They are scheduled checks that almost always pass.

## Data and features

* Training pairs: 73,934 (state after an inspection paired with the next routine inspection's outcome)
* Features use only information available at the time: past inspection counts, smoothed and recency-weighted infraction rates, results and infraction categories of recent inspections, inspection frequency, the City's minimum-inspections tier (pre-2024 establishments), establishment category, chain size
* Pass/fail history comes from the current feed only (the historical archive barely records Conditional Pass / Closed); severity history goes back to 2001 for linked establishments

## Out-of-time validation

Trained on targets from 2023-11-10 to 2025-11-11 (44,214 rows) and evaluated on 2025-11-12 to 2026-11-12 (29,720 rows). Lower Brier / log loss is better; ROC AUC 0.5 = no better than chance.

## Risk bands

Bands use the predicted chance of a significant/crucial infraction relative to the recent city-wide rate (21.7%): Low < 0.75x, Average 0.75 to 1.25x, Elevated 1.25 to 2x, High >= 2x.

Current counts: Low: 9,316, Average: 5,216, Elevated: 3,469, High: 797

## Limitations

* These are statistical estimates from past inspection records, **not** official ratings. The official result is the latest inspection status posted by Toronto Public Health.
* Establishments that close permanently disappear from the City's feed, so the training data over-represents survivors.
* Inspection practice changed with the late-2025 system migration (Conditional Pass rates went up). Recent data is weighted more heavily (one-year half-life), but some drift remains.
* Categories for establishments without an archive record are inferred from their name (grouped cross-validated accuracy 89% on confident predictions).
